KNITMEMO SUPPORT

A little record.
Every stitch.

A local journal for knitting and crochet. Organize your yarn, keep track of progress, and look back on the things you made.

Start your journal

  1. Add yarn, colors, and photos to Yarn Stash.
  2. Tap “+” to create a project, choose yarn, and set a counter unit.
  3. Open the project to count and add progress photos or a short note.
  4. Finish it with a photo, completion date, and yarn usage.

Keep your records safe

Choose Settings → Export ZIP Backup to save your records, photos, and PDFs together.

Save the ZIP outside the app before changing devices or removing the app. To restore it, choose Import Backup in Settings.

Import creates copies without overwriting existing records. KnitMemo does not automatically upload or sync across devices.

Common questions

Are my records or photos uploaded?

No. The app stores content on your device and has no accounts, ads, or analytics services. Copies leave the app only when you choose to export or share them to a location or service.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings from the journal page and choose English or 简体中文. The interface updates immediately.

How do I view a PDF pattern?

Select a PDF from Files when creating or editing a project. Once saved, you can open it from the project.

What if a photo is missing or an import fails?

Keep the original photos and your backup. Check that the files open in Photos or Files and that your device has available space. Avoid uninstalling the app to troubleshoot. If the issue continues, contact us below.

Contact the developer

For help or suggestions, open a GitHub Issue (a GitHub account is required). Include the app version, device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue.

Get support

Issues are public. Do not post private photos, full backups, passwords, or other sensitive content.

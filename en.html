A little help

Where do I start?

Add your yarn in Yarn Stash, then tap “+” to create a project and start your journal.

How do I back up or change devices?

Export a ZIP backup in Settings. On your other device, open KnitMemo and choose Import Backup.

Can I add PDF patterns?

Yes. Import a PDF when creating a project or editing its details, then open it from the project.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings → App Language and choose English or 简体中文.

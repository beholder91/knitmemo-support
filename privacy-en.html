KNITMEMO

Privacy Policy

Updated October 29, 2026

Data and collection

KnitMemo is a local knitting and crochet journal. The developer does not collect, receive, or sell records, photos, PDFs, identity, or usage data from the app. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking.

On-device storage

Yarn, projects, counters, journal entries, and imported photos and PDFs are stored in the app sandbox. Language and custom colors are stored in local preferences. The app does not automatically upload or sync this content. Device backups are managed through your Apple settings.

Photo and file access

The app uses the camera or reads selected photos and files only when you choose to add them to your journal. You can manage camera and photo permissions in system Settings.

Backups and sharing

A ZIP backup containing records, photos, and PDFs is created only when you export it. You choose where to save or share it. The privacy policies of those services apply to copies you give them. Keep backups safe and check their private content before sharing.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you confirm their deletion in the app or remove the app. There is no developer-held server copy. Exported ZIPs, original photos, original PDFs, and system backups are not automatically removed by deleting content in the app; manage those copies separately.

Support and this website

If you choose to contact the developer through GitHub Issues, your GitHub username, feedback, and attachments are publicly visible as submitted. The developer uses this information only to handle your feedback. Do not post sensitive content. Use GitHub’s features to edit or delete your feedback, or contact the developer in the original issue. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. Visits to this site and use of GitHub Issues are subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site adds no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies.

Updates and contact

This page will be updated if the app’s data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the developer through GitHub Issues.

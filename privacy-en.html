Privacy policy

Your knitting journal stays on your device.

Updated October 31, 2026

Records and photos

Yarn, projects, counters, journal entries, photos, and PDFs are stored in the app sandbox on your device. Language and custom colors are saved in local preferences. The developer does not collect, receive, or sell this content.

Permissions and backups

The camera, photos, and files are used only when you choose to add content. You can manage permissions in system Settings and export ZIP backups or share files yourself. System backups follow your Apple settings. Exported copies are managed by the destination or service you choose.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app. The developer holds no server copy. Exported backups, original photos, original PDFs, and system backups can be managed where they are saved.

Website and feedback

KnitMemo has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security. Feedback and attachments submitted through GitHub Issues are public and used to handle your questions and suggestions.

Contact and updates

This page will be updated if data practices change. You can contact the developer through GitHub Issues for privacy questions.
